## Description (4000 max)
Rally brings women's sports into one place. See what's on today, tomorrow and all week across the leagues you follow, and never lose track of your teams.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- Today, Tomorrow and Upcoming: every game across all your leagues, with live scores and big, easy-to-read start times.
- Follow your teams: pick the teams you love and their games pin to the top, whenever they play.
- Game pages: live scores, scoring plays, match events with goals, cards and substitutions, team stats, lineups and box scores.
- Teams and players: schedules, results, rosters, season stats and nationality.
- Top 25 first: college basketball and volleyball lists lead with the ranked teams.

LEAGUES AND COMPETITIONS
WNBA, NWSL, NCAA women's basketball and volleyball, Women's Super League and England's cups, UEFA Women's Champions League, Liga F, Première Ligue, and national teams including the Women's World Cup, Euros, Nations League and Concacaf competitions.

NO ACCOUNT NEEDED
Use Rally right away. Sign in with just your email, no password, only if you want your followed teams to sync across your devices. You can delete your account any time.

NO BETTING, EVER
Rally never shows betting odds, links to betting sites or gambling ads.

Rally is an independent app and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any league, team or school shown.

## App Review notes
Rally works fully without signing in. Sign-in is optional and only syncs followed teams across devices.
